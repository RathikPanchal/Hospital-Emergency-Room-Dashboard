# Hospital-Emergency-Room-Dashboard
Interactive Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard for analyzing patient admissions, age distribution, waiting times, patient satisfaction, gender demographics, and department referrals using KPI-driven data visualization.
🏥 Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

The Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard is an interactive data analytics project designed to monitor emergency room operations and analyze patient-related information. It presents key performance indicators (KPIs) and visual insights to help understand patient flow, admission patterns, waiting times, satisfaction scores, and department referrals.

The dashboard includes interactive month and year filters, KPI cards, charts, and trend visualizations for exploring hospital emergency room data.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze the total number of patients visiting the emergency room.

Compare admitted and non-admitted patients.

Understand patient age distribution across different age groups.

Monitor patient waiting times and timeliness of care.

Analyze patient satisfaction scores.

Explore gender-wise patient distribution.

Identify departments receiving the highest number of referrals.

📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

1. Number of Patients

Monitor the total number of patients and observe changes in patient volume.

2. Patient Admission Status

Compare admitted and non-admitted patients to understand admission patterns.

3. Patient Age Distribution

Group patients into age ranges to analyze demographic patterns.

4. Timeliness of Patient Care

Analyze the percentage of patients attended to within 30 minutes to evaluate the timeliness of care.

5. Average Waiting Time

Track average waiting time in minutes to identify potential delays.

6. Patient Satisfaction Score

Monitor patient satisfaction scores to understand service quality trends.

7. Gender Analysis

Visualize the number and percentage of male and female patients.

8. Department Referrals

Analyze referral counts across departments to identify the most frequently referred-to departments.

📈 Dashboard Features

Interactive monthly filtering.

Year selection for exploring different reporting periods.

KPI cards for quick performance monitoring.

Charts for admission status and gender analysis.

Age-group distribution visualization.

Department referral analysis.

Trend sparklines for patient volume, waiting time, and satisfaction.

💡 Key Insights

The dashboard helps users explore:

The balance between admitted and non-admitted patients.

Patient distribution across age groups and genders.

Differences between on-time and delayed patient attendance.

Waiting-time and patient satisfaction trends.

Departments receiving the highest number of referrals.
